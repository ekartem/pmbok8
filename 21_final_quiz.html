Final Assessment — PMBOK 8 Comprehensive

20 questions · tap an answer to check

PMBOK 8 has how many principles, performance domains, focus areas, and processes?

6-7-5-40 is the structural signature of PMBOK 8.

The three mindset dimensions are:

Proactive, ownership, value-driven — the mindset that wraps everything else.

Which principle replaced v7's 'Stewardship' and 'Leadership'?

Stewardship and leadership were consolidated into Be an Accountable Leader in v8.

How many processes does the Governance domain have?

Governance has 9 processes — the most of any domain — spread across all 5 Focus Areas.

Which domain has the fewest processes?

Schedule has just 3 processes: Plan Schedule Management, Develop Schedule, Monitor and Control Schedule.

The power/interest matrix is a tool for which domain?

The power × interest matrix determines stakeholder engagement strategy — manage closely, keep satisfied, keep informed, or monitor.

Forecast-to-complete is most associated with which domain?

Forecast-to-complete is the Finance domain's forward-looking indicator — where you&#39;re headed, not where you've been.

Resource leveling and smoothing differ in that:

Leveling moves dates; smoothing adds capacity. Choose based on which constraint is actually fixed.

PMBOK 8 lists how many threat response strategies?

Per PMBOK 8 Guide §5: five threat strategies are Escalate, Avoid, Transfer, Mitigate, Accept. Opportunities mirror them: Escalate, Exploit, Share, Enhance, Accept.

Focus Areas were formerly known as:

Same five groupings (Initiating through Closing), renamed to reflect that today's projects often satisfy them through informal practice, not just formal processes.

PMBOK 8's tailoring section was promoted from:

Tailoring was important enough to graduate from principle to dedicated section — it&#39;s the meta-skill of PMBOK 8.

Enterprise environmental factors (EEFs) are best described as:

Market conditions, regulations, culture, politics — EEFs constrain every decision.

A WBS is produced by which Scope process?

Develop Scope Structure decomposes the total scope into the hierarchical WBS.

Communications Management processes belong to which domain?

Plan Communications, Manage Communications, and Monitor Communications are all Stakeholder domain processes — 3 of its 7 total.

In PMBOK 8, where are quality requirements primarily addressed within the performance domains?

Quality remains a principle in v8 (Embed Quality Into Processes and Deliverables). Within the domains, quality requirements are handled by Scope processes — Define Scope identifies them, Monitor and Control Scope monitors them.

The project measurement baseline consists of:

Three baselines: scope, schedule, cost. Variance is measured against these.

Lead the Team is an explicit process in which domain?

Lead the Team is a Resources domain process in the Executing focus area. Team leadership is a process you run, not a personality trait.

Contingency reserve vs management reserve:

Confusing these is a common budget-reporting error. Contingency is for identified risks; management reserve is for surprises.

The correct sequence of PMBOK 8's framework layers from top to bottom is:

Mindset wraps principles, which guide domains, which use focus-area processes, all tailored. Top to bottom.

Under PMBOK 8, a project that delivers every committed deliverable but doesn&#39;t move the intended business outcome has:

The fundamental objective is value. Output without outcome = governance failed to maintain value orientation.
